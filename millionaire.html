Who Wants to Be a Brewmaster?

Climb 15 questions from the BBMB 1200 lectures, each tougher than the last, to win a (pretend) $1,000,000.

  • Safe havens at $1,000 (Q5) and $32,000 (Q10): miss after reaching one and you keep it.
  • Walk away any time to keep what you've won.
  • Three lifelines, each usable once per game:
    • 50:50 removes two wrong answers.
    • Ask the Class polls a simulated lecture hall. They're right less often on harder questions.
    • Swap Question trades the question for a new one of the same difficulty.
  • Each game draws from a bank of questions, and you'll see new ones before any repeats.
